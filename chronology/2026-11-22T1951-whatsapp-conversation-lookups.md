# When the answer spans several WhatsApp messages

The owner asked whether ordinary Idli terms could recover a document posted after a reply, a website mentioned by “V”, meeting times and posted lists. We froze seven questions and ran them through the unchanged CLI on a fresh active-group snapshot. All three configurations missed every complete answer. Direct CLI searches could locate evidence; shortening the menu helped the 9B reach WhatsApp more often, but it mistook the timestamp of a message for the time of the call. Clef recognised concrete proposals in a small, separately judged sample. A group-local cache check found the quoted parent missing from the ordinary store view.

Before adding a verb, compare resolving a group or person and fetching a small reply or time window against semantic search on fresh cases. Keep private transcripts local. [Cases, source checks, logs and results](../benchmarks/runs/2026-11-22-whatsapp-lookup/README.md)
