# An explicit group cue changed the selected source

The owner asked whether the mapper could see WhatsApp. The recorded menu listed the two WhatsApp views and their columns. Three natural source cues selected the group messages for the same question that otherwise went to mail. All missed the proposed names. The linked browser's native local search also missed known positive controls. A phone-side check can distinguish missing browser history from a word-choice problem.
