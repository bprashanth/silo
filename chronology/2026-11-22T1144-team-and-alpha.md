# Two ways into the same CLI

The owner distinguished teammates reproducing Silo on laptops from alpha testers who will only see Signal. A laptop can finish Google's existing loopback consent during its first sync. A tester opening a link on a phone needs a hosted callback bound to their Signal identity. The existing one-person bridge gives us a working conversation to test against. The next deployment question is whether two people can connect, sync and revoke independently.

We also agreed to leave a trail from a readable conclusion to the conversation and the actual experiment. The [Muse field note](../narrative/muse-harness.md) uses an existing run to show how. For this rollout see [team setup](../deployment/team/README.md) and the [alpha acceptance checks](../deployment/alpha/signal.md).
