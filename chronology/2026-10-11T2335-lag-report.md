# "Everything is lagging": what it was

The user's screenshot (23:28) showed:
- four 1-second voice notes from the bot that wouldn't download (✕);
- their own 4 s voice note stuck with a clock icon;
- "Updating…" in the chat header.

**Bot side, checked at 23:29.** Telegram had nothing queued for the bot
(`pending_update_count` 0), and the box reached the API in about 0.5 s. The bot never saw
the voice note: it hadn't left the phone. "Updating…" is Telegram's client failing to connect. When
the phone reconnected, the note arrived ("remind me to wake up at 7am tomorrow") and was
handled in seconds. The 1-second voice notes were Read aloud of very short messages, most
likely tapped several times while nothing seemed to happen.

**The bot was also slow by design, and that's fixed:**
- **Small talk:** it went through Codex plus the checker (about 20 s for "thanks"). The todo
  router now has a `small_talk` tool and answers greetings and thanks in the same local call.
  "thanks!" took 7 s end to end over Telegram.
- **Voice notes:** the transcript (🎙️ "…") is sent the moment Whisper finishes, before the answer.
- **Data questions:** the bot sends "⏳ Working on it… usually 30–90 seconds" at once, instead of
  only the typing indicator.
- **Read aloud:** a repeat tap on the same message within 2 minutes is ignored ("Already sent").
  Each read aloud is logged with its time.

**Still true:** a real data question takes 30–90 s (Codex plus the independent check), and a
phone with a poor connection makes any Telegram bot look frozen.
