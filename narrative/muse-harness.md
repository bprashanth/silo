# What did the harness change for Muse?

In October we ran the same local Muse model through two harnesses on spreadsheet questions. Across two trials on 67 questions, io Codex answered 131 of 134 correctly and the simple harness answered 125. Some misses came from date interpretation or counting blank rows. An answer could repeat a number straight from tool output and still count the wrong thing.

That gives us a reason to check the underlying selection when evaluating an answer about a spreadsheet. The comparison used the earlier Telegram-era stack. Today's Signal and 9B paths need their own measurements.
